## Repair proof

- `SurfelGI.indirectLight` samples the game-owned GBuffer albedo and multiplies it by the
  integrated radiance written by the BVH trace. It no longer uses flags or coverage as lighting.
- `SurfelPool` keeps `active` as the CPU-owned residency mask. GPU-owned `flags` and `radiance`
  are never CPU-cleared during allocation, ageing, or expiry; the compute node skips inactive
  lanes before tracing.
- `createGBuffer()` adds an owned albedo attachment without aliasing SSR's metalness attachment.
- Minimal `Play.ts` constructs GI lazily from the composition line, uses a 64-lane budget so the
  compact scene's seeded floor and wall vertices are integrated, and reports the asynchronous GPU
  radiance through the game-owned wall colour after `KeyC`.
