## Repair proof

- `SurfelGI.indirectLight` reconstructs a pixel world position from the GBuffer depth, resolves
  its hash cell, and reads the corresponding GPU `radiance` entries gated by cell occupancy, GPU
  hit flags, and the CPU active mask. It does not consume GBuffer albedo as a lighting multiplier.
- `SurfelIntegrator` traces the GPU BVH and writes the game-provided lighting expression into the
  radiance buffer. The package supplies only neutral state/geometry plumbing; direction,
  attenuation, normal response, strength, and radiance are explicit game-owned inputs.
- `SurfelPool` keeps `active` as the CPU-owned residency mask. GPU-owned `flags` and `radiance` are
  never CPU-cleared during ageing or expiry. `SurfelGI` repopulates/reallocates BVH-backed lanes
  before expiry and dispatches the changed residency immediately.
- `SurfelHashGrid` uses the configured cell size and count in both CPU rebuild/query and TSL
  integration lookup. Its fixed buffers are rebuilt and marked dirty without growing.
- `createGBuffer()` adds an owned albedo attachment without aliasing SSR's metalness attachment.
- The minimal template constructs `SurfelGI` only through the composition callback. Its render
  source owns the direction, attenuation, strength, wall-colour radiance, and composite multiplier.
  The playtest reads `ctx.renderer.readback(gi.pool.radiance.value)` and averages active lanes;
  it does not synthesize a bounce from coverage or authored colour.

`giCoverage` and `giBounceRed` stayed `0`; the positive GI assertions failed with
