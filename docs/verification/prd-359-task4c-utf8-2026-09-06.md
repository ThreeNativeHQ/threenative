## Scope

The fallback `TextDecoder` now implements strict UTF-8 decoding for the protocol:
`fatal: true` throws on malformed bytes, nonfatal decoding emits U+FFFD, streaming
decoding retains split multibyte tails, typed-array views honor their offsets, and
unsupported labels/options fail honestly. `TextDecoderStream` forwards the decoder
options and flushes its final pending sequence.
