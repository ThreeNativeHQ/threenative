  crate landing on the goal pad so the win condition could fire with no player involvement.

  proof axis the framework loses, 54 to 58. Recorded in `docs/verification/score-physics-puzzle-2026-08-14.md`.
- Two further defects were fixed to make the round runnable at all: the plain-Three.js bridge could
  never advertise `runtime.physics`, and a created-but-never-scaffolded sandbox could be cleared by
  neither `pnpm sandbox` nor `pnpm sweep:archive`. Both are in PRD-107.
- Open engine question, unresolved and not guessed at: the builder measured that its `Area3D` never
  reported the `CharacterBody3D`, yet the platformer template's `Pickup` area fires with a
  `CharacterBody3D` player and its `collect` playtest passes. `areaIntersections` uses
  `world.intersectionsWithShape`, which bypasses Rapier's `ActiveCollisionTypes`, so the obvious
  kinematic-pair explanation is wrong. Note that the platformer's `Pickup` handler takes no body
  argument, so its passing test does not prove the player is what entered. This needs a targeted
  experiment before anyone writes a fix.
