## The numbers

| Pass | Status | Inputs | Cost | Share of wall clock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ktx2` | ran | 165 | 909,061 ms (~15.2 min) | 38% |
| `model` | ran | 165 | 1,377,738 ms (~23.0 min) | 58% |
| — | — | — | **total 2374.2 s** | passes = 96% |

Per-asset attribution inside `model` (the rows a 274-file pack needs): the four heaviest inputs
were `UnrealMaterialLibrary.glb` 45,587 ms, `SM_BoughGroup01.glb` 35,971 ms,
`SM_BoughGroup02.glb` 33,050 ms, `SM_BoughGroup03.glb` 31,662 ms — three bough groups cost
100 s together, and each ran while every other core sat idle. `ktx2` per-asset rows are 0 ms for
GLB inputs (nothing standalone to encode) and carry the texture costs; per-input rows are sorted
by logical path in the report itself.

The two passes account for 2287 s of the 2374 s — the bake is sequential on two WASM-heavy
passes, which is exactly the claim PRD-319 tests: a 24-thread machine ran one encoder at a time.

## Secondary subject — repo fixtures

`test-support/fixtures/` (1 skinned character GLB): first bake 1.1 s total, `model` 403 ms cold;
warm in-process bakes 20–22 ms (WASM init is the cold cost). Two bakes of unchanged input report
byte-stable cost rows apart from durations, and the second bake reports every pass `cached` with
`durationMs 0` from the cache decision, not from a threshold.

## Noise band (AC7)

Cold bakes pay ~300 ms of WASM/Basis initialisation inside the first texture encode (0.82 s total
for the fixture subject run 1 vs 0.02–0.03 s warm). The instrument adds two `performance.now()`
reads per `apply` — orders of magnitude below that band. On the wildwood subject the instrument's
overhead is noise against a 2374 s bake.

## Findings the bake surfaced (both fail-closed, both real marketplace content)

1. **`TN_ASSETS_PASS_FAILED`: a 4096×4096 normal map exceeds the Basis encoder's 12 Mpix limit**
   (`T_cliffrock01_normal.png`). With defaults, the wildwood pack cannot compile at all. A game
   must declare `textures.maxSize` — the cap resizes before encode.
2. **`TN_ASSETS_TEXTURE_BLOCK_SIZE`: three images are not multiples of the 4×4 block** — an 11×10
   and a 92×2 data map in the FAB listing and one 1024×205 atlas in the game's own landscape set.
   Each needs a `codec: "none"` override. All three were scanned for and named; the full pack scan
   found exactly three (3 of 88 images).

## What PRD-319 owns

Same machine, same input, same declared config. Its speedup gate reads these rows; its
correctness gate diffs the compiled output against this bake's `passCosts`-carrying result and
the content-addressed outputs. Baseline total to beat: **2374.2 s**.
