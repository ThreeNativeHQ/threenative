## Visual and platform notes

The screenshot capture was inspected under Xvfb: the dungeon floor and walls, teal player, red
enemy, gold dungeon light, and React HUD panels are visible. `sky.ts` is intentionally not a
visible outdoor sky: its dark back-sided dome supplies the interior ambient envelope and fog
