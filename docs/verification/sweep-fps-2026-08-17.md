## What this ledger is saying

Rows 4, 12, 17 and 18 are one defect in four places: **the generated `AGENTS.md` and the
generated `package.json` describe a project that does not behave the way they say.** The
documented step spelling silently does not advance the clock, the documented resource id is not
the one the sealed proof reads, the scenarios the document says you may delete are wired into a
script that breaks when you delete them, and the smoke test the template ships cannot pass as
generated on this machine.

Rows 1, 3, 9 and 10 are the genre meeting a framework shaped around a third-person platformer: no
mouse-look input, an odometer that reads zero because the character transform is written after
the frame, and a pick API that cannot express a hitscan shot. **Row 1 is the most serious**,
because its workaround is explicitly a portability violation — the framework's own rules say a
game must not reach for `document`, and building a first-person camera left no alternative, so
this build's look control is web-only by construction.

Row 13 is against the capture harness this experiment supplied, not against the framework.
