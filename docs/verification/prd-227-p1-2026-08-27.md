# PRD-227 P1 — 2026-08-27

## Result

P1 **passes after re-derivation**. The final packed stream reduced median `bridgeNs` from 9.31 ms
to 0.81 ms and median `work` from 23.19 ms to 14.32 ms. Both pre-registered thresholds pass, so
Phase 2 may start.

| Arm | work | bridge | bridge overhead | command |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Direct-call control | 23.19 ms | 9.31 ms | 3.87 ms | 1.84 ms |
| Packed-stream candidate | 14.32 ms | 0.81 ms | 0.19 ms | 2.32 ms |
| Change | -8.87 ms | -8.50 ms | -3.67 ms | +0.48 ms |

The initial candidate missed at 21.70 ms of work even though its seam crossing collapsed. That pair
did not price serialization. `jsFrameNs` is a desktop thread-CPU interval around the rAF callback, including
recorder writes and remaining bridge work but excluding the packed-buffer drain and C++ replay. The
PRD's 10.1 ms retained-JavaScript assumption came from a different Pixel 8 sampled profile, so
subtracting it from the candidate's 14.83 ms mixes platforms and marker semantics.

On this desktop pair, control `jsFrameNs - bridgeNs` was 16.57 ms and candidate was 13.78 ms. The
candidate's descriptive post/pre-rAF residual was 6.09 ms, including 4.06 ms of selected replay
commands and 1.88 ms of drain, unmetered replay, profiler emission, and loop work. These medians are
not additive. A V8 CPU profile over 300 rendered frames attributed 2.98% of samples to the largest
anonymous recorder site, 1.91% to `emit`, 1.35% to `ensure`, and 0.80% to recorded `writeBuffer`.

The corrected candidate restored upload staging and disabled the test-only operation-name trace in
production. In a new eight-launch direct-versus-packed pair, work improved from 27.18 ms to 18.95 ms
and bridge cost from 10.67 ms to 0.99 ms. This recovered 2.75 ms from the initial candidate, but it
still missed the 17 ms ceiling by 1.95 ms.

Profile-only markers then measured packed-buffer drain at 0.069 ms and total native replay at
3.22 ms over 1,471 eligible frames. Reusing a 1 MiB JavaScript arena, returning its declared prefix
without a final copy, and removing the upload view's redundant `.slice()` reduced drain to 0.003 ms.
A six-launch packed-candidate A/B measured work at 19.27 ms before and 18.35 ms after, a 0.92 ms
improvement with bridge and command time flat. Row `91-frame-upload-stream` retained exact pass
status. The fresh direct-versus-arena acceptance pair then passed at 14.32 ms work and 0.81 ms
bridge.

One subsequent parser experiment replaced the render-pass hash map with frame-local slots. Its
six-launch A/B regressed work from 19.02 ms to 19.80 ms, so it was reverted. A typed-u32 recorder
probe was also slower in its standalone screen and was reverted before commit.
